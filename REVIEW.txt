IMPLEMENTED BEHAVIOR

- The gameplay HUD shows only the native next-item icon and its soul amount.
  Extra previews (items 2-5 and their soul labels) are hidden, including when
  Universal Mod Manager has a saved Items shown value above 1. The next
  icon's count labels are hidden; its background, readiness effects, and
  position are retained.
- Queue rows retain the normal soul icon and item price. The added slash
  and cumulative amount are hidden in the item entry's own stylesheet.
- The top shop box is labeled Total Cost and uses the original full-queue
  calculation, including upgrades and estimated sale credits. Current held
  souls do not reduce that total. An empty queue displays zero unless the
  box is disabled in settings.
- The gameplay HUD amount remains:
  max(0, next purchase price - current souls - estimated queued-sale proceeds).
  No separate next-item amount is displayed in the shop.
- The bottom total box is removed entirely. Its creation code, visibility
  handling, footer space reservation, and Clear Queue width limit are gone.
- Existing pricing functions, the 0.1-second refresh, and native purchase,
  sale, removal, reorder, and clear handlers are retained.

LIMITATIONS

The tests use mocked Panorama APIs and a limited CSS selector matcher.
They do not verify native rendering, font metrics, scrolling, overlap at
different UI scales, automatic icon advancement, or other HUD mods.

Owned components: pricing reads the game's ModCost label. Tests supply a
discounted native price; they do not prove that the live game supplies the
expected discount in every upgrade case. No second discount is applied.

Recipes and sales: the inherited upgrade mappings use English item names
and can become stale after game updates. Sale estimates remain half the
displayed price, rounded down; full-refund windows are not modeled.

Number formatting: the inherited parser expects integer-formatted labels.
It does not correctly parse abbreviations such as 1.6k. Native resources
inspected during development used integer placeholders; the parity test
does not establish support for abbreviated values.

Settings: the Items shown slider remains exposed but cannot restore extra
HUD previews in this build. Total queue cost in shop controls the top box
using its saved value. The obsolete Souls needed in shop option is removed;
its legacy saved key is still accepted for hidden row labels but cannot
control the total box or gameplay HUD amount.

Source maps: preserved CSS mappings cover the original text, not appended
rules. This limits source-level diagnostics for the added styles.

IN-GAME CHECKLIST

1. Queue an 800-soul item with 300 souls. The top Total Cost should show 800;
   close the shop and confirm the gameplay HUD amount is 500.
2. Add a queued sale priced at 400. Its 200-soul estimate should reduce
   Total Cost to 600 and the gameplay HUD amount to 300.
3. Increase held souls to 600: Total Cost should remain 600 while the HUD
   amount reaches 0. Clear the queue: the top total should show 0.
4. Reorder, remove, buy, and sell items. Confirm the values update and check
   an upgrade against its actual price with an owned component.
5. Fill and scroll a long queue. Confirm row prices have no fraction, there
   is no bottom total box, and Clear Queue has its normal space.
6. Close the shop with at least three items queued. Only the next icon and
   its soul amount should appear beside the item slots, with no count over
   the icon. Buy/remove the first item and confirm the icon advances.
7. Reopen the shop. Confirm the full queue and single top Total Cost box.
   Toggle Total queue cost in shop and confirm it controls that box.
