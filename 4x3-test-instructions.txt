4:3 compatibility test

Keep your 4:3 HUD (pak48_dir.vpk) and replace only Enhanced Quickbuy with
this pak07_dir.vpk. Do not load the old and new Quickbuy VPKs together.

At 1280x960:
1. Open the shop. Confirm the Quick Buy and Total Cost cards have their
   backgrounds/icons and align above the queue, without covering the shop.
2. Add, reorder, and remove items; fill and scroll a long queue. Check that
   rows show normal prices without fractions and all buttons still work.
3. Close the shop. Confirm one next-item icon, its soul counter, and the
   ready-to-buy glow appear without a count or blue corner patch. Buy or
   remove the first item and confirm the next icon and amount update.
4. Confirm the 4:3 HUD's health, abilities, item slots, and shop layout
   remain usable. Also check the usual widescreen setup for regressions.

Quick price check: an 800-soul item with 300 held souls should show 500
on the gameplay HUD and 800 in the shop Total Cost box. A queued sale
priced at 400 contributes an estimated 200, making those amounts 300 and
600 respectively. Clear the queue and confirm the shop total is zero.

2- and 3-item versions (variants/2-items, variants/3-items)
Install one in place of pak07_dir.vpk. Run steps 5-9 at 1280x960 with
the 4:3 HUD, then again at your usual widescreen resolution without it.
Queue at least four items:
5. Close the shop. Confirm the 2-item file shows the next item plus one
   preview, and the 3-item file shows the next item plus two previews.
   The previews must not cover item slots, abilities, or other HUD.
6. Hold 300 souls and queue three different 800-soul items, none of
   which upgrades another, with no queued sales. From left to right the
   amounts should read 500, 1300, 2100. The 2-item file shows only 500
   and 1300. Queue one 800-soul item for sale: the amounts drop by 400.
7. Buy or remove the first item and confirm the previews shift left.
   Open the shop, die, or clear the queue and confirm the previews hide.
8. If Universal Mod Manager is installed, set Items shown to 1 and
   confirm only the next item remains. Set it to 5 and confirm the count
   does not exceed the version's 2 or 3 items.
9. Clear the queue. Queue one owned item for sale, then three items to
   buy. Note which item the large next-item icon shows and what each
   preview shows. Report whether the first buy item is missing from
   the icons. This behavior comes from the original mod and is untested.

Report the resolution and what passed or failed. If something looks wrong,
screenshots of both the open shop and the gameplay HUD will help locate it.

The maintainer reported the 1-item build (steps 1-4) working in-game on
2026-10-24. The 2- and 3-item versions (steps 5-9) are not yet tested.
This checklist remains available for regression testing of future changes.
